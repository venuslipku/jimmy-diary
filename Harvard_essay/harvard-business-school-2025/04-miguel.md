# Miguel's Essay

**Collection:** Successful Harvard Business School Essays (2025)  
**Source:** <https://business.thecrimson.com/successful-harvard-business-school-application-essays-2025>  
**Essay #:** 4

## Prompt

Growth-Oriented Essay Prompt: "Curiosity can be seen in many ways. Please share an example of how you have demonstrated curiosity and how that has influenced your growth

## Essay

My favorite place in the world is Lopresti Park, a soccer field in East Boston. Here, you might see an Algerian dancing to Brazilian funk music or a Nigerian sharing Colombian food with an Albanian. Despite our differences in language, culture, and religion, we’re united by the same passion: soccer. Growing up here taught me that soccer is a universal language that transcends barriers and allows communities to form across lines that often divide. I knew that the sport could be used on a greater scale.

So, I decided to conduct research in Colombia on soccer’s role in social transformation in marginalized communities. After seeing the sport used as a tool for reconciliation in Medellín and Bogotá, I applied what I learned by organizing tournaments that brought together over 40 people. During one match, two players got into a scuffle, and I had to step in. I paused the game, mirrored their words, validated their frustrations, and reframed the issue to help them reconcile. Weeks later, I witnessed one of those players—known for being hot-headed—step in to de-escalate a similar dispute. I felt I had made a difference in my neighborhood.

My curiosity, which stemmed from seeing my community being formed across barriers, led me to understand the importance of building understanding between those from different backgrounds. As someone whose life has oscillated between disparate cultural and socioeconomic worlds, I’ve often served as a bridge between people from different backgrounds. At HBS, I’ll bring this same spirit. I’ll contribute to the Private Equity Club, Impact Investing Club, and Consulting for Impact with my global perspective and experience from Fulcrum. I’ll join clubs like Soccer, First Generation, and the LASO. But above all, I’ll strive to build bridges and facilitate a compassionate community.

## Professional Review by Personal MBA Coach

Miguel’s approach to HBS’s final essay, which remains the only unchanged prompt for the 2025 to 2026 MBA application cycle, is both smart and strategic. Many applicants have expressed concerns to Personal MBA Coach that the new HBS essays offer limited space to share their personal stories and tell HBS what truly sets them apart. Through this curiosity prompt, however, Miguel effectively conveys multiple distinct messages to the admissions committee, each thoughtfully woven into his narrative.

First and foremost, by sharing how he researched soccer’s broader impact on marginalized communities and then used this research to benefit his own community, Miguel conveys his passion for soccer along with his intellectual curiosity, deep appreciation for diverse cultures and backgrounds, and a proclivity for action. These values all resonate strongly with HBS.

Second, Miguel presents himself as a leader in an authentic and understated way. Rather than focusing on his own accolades, he tells a story about how another player benefited from the skills Miguel taught him. This approach allows readers to see Miguel as a leader others naturally want to follow.

Finally, Miguel shares his distinctive cultural and socioeconomic background, positioning himself as a bridge between different communities. This insight helps the admissions team envision the inclusive and thoughtful leader he will be on campus.
Despite this essay’s many strengths, it could be improved by removing the closing section on HBS. Personal MBA Coach does not advise including long lists of contributions, as these often come across as disingenuous and not well developed. Further, this type of content is not necessary to address the prompt.
